The commodity layer

Everyone can use the same models. What makes the result yours is not the model but what you give it to work with: your structure, your data and your rules.

What it is

AI is the reasoning layer: the model that reads, writes, summarises and proposes. It is powerful, and it is the same for you as for your neighbour or your competitor. A new model comes out every few months, and the old advantage disappears with it.

Why it matters

If the model is the same for everyone, the difference has to come from somewhere else. It comes from the context the model gets and from the checks on what it produces. A model pointed at a well-structured vault, with clear instructions and rules, gives better answers than a stronger model pointed at a pile.

This also keeps you free. When the structure is yours and in plain files, you can switch to a better or cheaper model without rebuilding anything.

What it looks like

At home. I use an AI assistant that works directly in my vault. It reads an instruction file at the root, follows the folder conventions and uses small skills for repeated jobs. The model has changed several times; the vault has not had to.

At work. The same approach means investing in the data model, the metadata and the governance around AI, and treating the model itself as a replaceable supplier.

The usual mistake

Shopping for the best model instead of building the best context. A better prompt or a newer model gives a short bump. A better structure lifts every answer from then on.
